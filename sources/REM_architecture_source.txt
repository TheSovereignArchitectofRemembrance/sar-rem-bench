2.2 What the public testing actually demonstrates
As of this audit, the X corpus demonstrates protocol, not proof.
• It demonstrates that SAR can freeze a hypothesis before looking at outcomes (atmospheric transition 
test v0.2 / v0.3).
• It demonstrates willingness to subtract primitives (chassis hold; incidence ≠ composability ≠ memory).
• It demonstrates a reconstruction law: observation yields a set Γ, not a unique recovered history.
• It does not yet demonstrate out-of-sample predictive lift over established baselines in any locked 
domain.
• It does not make the 246-page cosmological claims empirically current. Those remain historical-pattern 
reconstructions with a stated limited scope.
That last sentence is the cleanest service this audit can give the work. Promoting the white paper’s collapse 
windows, 72-year distortion cycle, or 2029–2038 corridor as if they had passed the later Γ-contraction standard 
would violate SAR’s own 2026 contract.

4.1 Field-Stroke Kernel v0.1 — substrate
Source render: SAR Field-Stroke Kernel v0.1. Substrate before throat and tongue.
This is the strongest piece in the corpus. It does not ask the reader to believe in a forgotten civilization. It states 
a reconstruction contract:
• Preserve the raw stroke γ(t). Never discard it because a later story is prettier.
• A field F is the set of admissible transitions, not the journey itself.
• Segmentation Σ is an operation. Same stroke, different cuts, different grammars. Record the cut.
• Observers are off-axis. They are not the stroke, not the field, not the operator.
• Recovery is set-valued. Goal is not to guess the one true path. Goal is to narrow the set lawfully.
GT-001 (same final trace, two journeys) is the right primitive example. Topology or final shape can survive while 
generative history does not. That is already enough to discipline every later glyph, myth, and letter-as-orbit 
claim.

5. Independent subtraction — what survives
The 31 August chassis-hold asked for entailment, permission, backfill, redundancy, missing primitives, 
dimensional error, and closure failure. This brief records the smallest honest return without repairing the 
architecture for comfort.
Survives
• Distinction + admissible relation is enough to talk about constraint as a restriction of a relation set.
• A realized sequence of admissible transitions can be called a path. That does not yet give geometry, 
force, or memory.
• Set-valued recovery: Γ(O|F,M) = {γ1, γ2, …}. New lawful observation must not enlarge the set.
• Segmentation-as-operation. Provenance must store cuts, not only the final object.
• Robust action under non-uniqueness. Act on invariants when identity fails.
Does not survive as currently stated
• Shared relatum (incidence) does not entail composability. Typing, roles, and admission conditions were 
being smuggled.
• Self-composability F:X→X is iterability, not recursion-as-memory.
• History-dependent composability is not yet Memory. Memory needs state, update, and counterfactual 
sensitivity specified.
• A 2-D enclosure drawing does not entail a 3-D coupled field. Projection crossings are the standard 
dimensional error.
• Operator-strings for H through B do not entail atomic architecture. They are labeled hypotheses and 
should stay that way until a null mapping is beaten.
Smallest remaining chassis
FIELD as a set of distinguishable possibilities; CONSTRAINT as restriction of admissible relations; STROKE as a 
realized trajectory; CUT as a recorded segmentation; OBSERVATION as a set-contractor; ANCHOR as what still 
holds across the surviving set. Observer stays off-axis. Memory is not primitive until an update rule is specified 
and tested.

7. Highest-information next moves
The 31 August packet asked for the single highest-information experiment. The answer is not another diagram.
1. Close or kill the atmospheric bridge. It is already pre-registered. Either it produces MCC lift over locked 
baselines or it becomes a clean failure in the REM ledger. That single outcome teaches more than another 
operator string.
2. Publish GT-001 as a worked numerical example. Same final drawing, two parameterizations, explicit Γ before 
and after one extra observation. This is the teaching object. It also answers today’s F/T/Y reconstruction thread: 
topology can survive; unique geometry cannot without retained hinges.
3. Freeze HGDE promotion criteria. Outperform random baselines, scrambled operator maps, and weak 
structural heuristics — the March 2026 release already said this. Run one frozen glyph set through that gauntlet 
and record the miss table.
4. Stop dual-use of the word Source. On X it is handle, method, metaphysical origin, and generative field. Pick 
four labeled senses and never let them silently trade places.
5. Keep Steve attached while he is carrying it. The 2 Sep instruction is accepted. Personality is provenance in a 
living reconstruction. Strip later if the architecture stands without the carrier. Do not strip first.
